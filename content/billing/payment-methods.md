# Payment Methods

QualityNode accepts a variety of payment methods for your convenience.

## Accepted Payment Methods

### Credit/Debit Cards
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover

### Digital Wallets
- PayPal
- Apple Pay (where available)
- Google Pay (where available)

### Cryptocurrency
- Bitcoin (BTC)
- Ethereum (ETH)
- USDC
- Other major cryptocurrencies via Coinbase Commerce

## Adding a Payment Method

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Click **Billing**
3. Click **Add Payment Method**
4. Enter your payment details
5. Save

## Updating Payment Information

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Click **Billing**
3. Find your payment method
4. Click **Edit**
5. Update details and save

## Removing a Payment Method

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Click **Billing**
3. Find the payment method to remove
4. Click **Remove**

> [!NOTE]
> You cannot remove a payment method if it's the only one on file and you have active subscriptions.

## Payment Security

- All transactions are encrypted with SSL/TLS
- Card details are stored securely with our payment processor
- We never store full card numbers on our servers
- PCI DSS compliant payment handling

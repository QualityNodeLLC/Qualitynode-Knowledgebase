# Refunds

Refund eligibility is governed by the terms presented for the purchase. This
knowledgebase does not create a separate eligibility window or processing
schedule.

## Request billing review

Open a billing ticket or email support@qualitynode.net with:

- Your account email
- The relevant invoice or order identifier
- The service involved
- A concise description of the billing issue

Do not include card numbers, passwords, or other payment credentials. Billing
support will provide the decision and any next steps for the specific purchase.

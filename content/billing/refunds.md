# Refunds

Our refund policy is designed to be fair and straightforward.

## Refund Policy

### 48-Hour Money-Back Guarantee

New customers can request a full refund within 48 hours of their first purchase, no questions asked.

### After 48 Hours

Refunds after the initial 48 hours are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. We consider:
- Service issues on our end
- Accidental duplicate purchases
- Extenuating circumstances

## What's Eligible

- First-time purchases within 48 hours
- Service outages or technical issues
- Accidental purchases
- Duplicate charges

## What's Not Eligible

- Servers used extensively before refund request
- Requests made after significant time has passed
- Cryptocurrency payments (due to irreversibility)
- Annual subscriptions after 7 days

## Requesting a Refund

1. Contact support via email or Discord
2. Include your account email
3. Specify the server/service
4. Explain the reason for your request

## Processing Time

- Credit card refunds: 5-10 business days
- PayPal refunds: 3-5 business days
- Other methods may vary

> [!NOTE]
> Refund processing time depends on your payment provider. QualityNode typically processes refund requests within 24-48 hours.

## Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer a partial refund or account credit:
- Pro-rated for time not used
- Applied as credit for future services

# Invoices

Access and download your billing history and invoices.

## Viewing Invoices

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Click **Billing History**
3. View all past invoices

## Invoice Details

Each invoice includes:
- Invoice number
- Date issued
- Services billed
- Payment method used
- Amount paid
- Tax information (where applicable)

## Downloading Invoices

1. Find the invoice in your billing history
2. Click **Download PDF**
3. Save for your records

> [!TIP]
> Download invoices for tax purposes or business expense tracking.

## Invoice Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Paid | Payment completed successfully |
| Pending | Awaiting payment |
| Failed | Payment attempt failed |
| Refunded | Payment has been refunded |

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails:

1. You'll receive an email notification
2. We'll retry the payment after 24 hours
3. Update your payment method if needed
4. Services may be suspended after multiple failures

## Need a Custom Invoice?

Contact support if you need:
- Company name on invoice
- VAT/Tax ID addition
- Special billing arrangements
- Consolidated invoices

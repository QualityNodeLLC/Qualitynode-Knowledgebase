# Upgrades and Downgrades

Review the [current plan comparison](/getting-started/choosing-a-plan), then open
the billing portal to see which changes are available for your subscription.

## Before confirming

- Verify the selected plan's RAM, CPU, and storage.
- Confirm that current disk usage fits the destination plan.
- Read the effective date and charge or credit summary shown by the portal.
- Back up important server data before reducing capacity.

The portal confirmation is authoritative for account-specific timing and cost.
If the required change is not available, open a billing ticket or email
support@qualitynode.net.

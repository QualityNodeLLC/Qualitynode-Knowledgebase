# Upgrades and Downgrades

Change your server plan at any time as your needs evolve.

## Upgrading Your Plan

Upgrading takes effect immediately:

1. Go to your server panel
2. Click **Settings**
3. Click **Change Plan**
4. Select your new plan
5. Review prorated charges
6. Confirm upgrade

### Proration

When upgrading:
- You're charged the difference for the remaining billing period
- Example: Upgrade from Agent ($9.99) to Director ($19.99) halfway through the month
- Charge: ~$5 for the remaining time

> [!TIP]
> Upgrades are instant. Your server will have more resources immediately after upgrading.

## Downgrading Your Plan

Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period:

1. Go to your server panel
2. Click **Settings**
3. Click **Change Plan**
4. Select a lower plan
5. Review the changes
6. Confirm downgrade

### Important Notes

- Downgrade happens at billing cycle end
- No immediate refund for unused time
- Ensure your data fits the new storage limit
- Some features may become unavailable

> [!WARNING]
> Before downgrading, verify your server data is within the new plan's storage limits to avoid data loss.

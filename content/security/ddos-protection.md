# DDoS Protection

All QualityNode servers include DDoS protection to keep your gaming experience uninterrupted.

## What is DDoS?

A Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack attempts to overwhelm your server with traffic, making it unavailable to legitimate players.

## Protection Levels

| Plan | Protection Level | Mitigation Capacity |
|------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Threshold | Basic | Up to 10 Gbps |
| Agent | Enhanced | Up to 40 Gbps |
| Director | Advanced | Up to 100 Gbps |
| Astral | Astral | Up to 400 Gbps |

## How It Works

1. **Traffic Analysis** - Incoming traffic is analyzed in real-time
2. **Threat Detection** - Malicious patterns are identified
3. **Filtering** - Bad traffic is filtered before reaching your server
4. **Clean Traffic** - Only legitimate traffic reaches your server

## Protection Features

### Always-On Protection
DDoS protection is active 24/7, not just during attacks.

### Automatic Mitigation
No manual intervention needed - attacks are mitigated automatically.

### Gaming-Optimized
Our protection is optimized for game server traffic, minimizing false positives.

### Zero Latency Impact
Protection adds minimal latency to legitimate connections.

## During an Attack

If your server is under attack:

1. Protection activates automatically
2. You may notice brief lag during mitigation
3. Attack traffic is filtered
4. Normal service resumes

> [!NOTE]
> Large attacks may cause brief service interruption while mitigation activates. This typically lasts only seconds.

## Reporting Attacks

If you believe you're under sustained attack:

1. Contact support immediately
2. Provide server details
3. Describe the symptoms
4. We'll investigate and adjust protection as needed

# Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account.

## What is 2FA?

2FA requires two forms of identification:
1. Something you know (your password)
2. Something you have (your phone)

Even if someone steals your password, they can't access your account without your phone.

## Setting Up 2FA

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Click **Security**
3. Click **Enable 2FA**
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app
5. Enter the verification code
6. Save your backup codes

## Recommended Authenticator Apps

- **Authy** - Works on multiple devices
- **Google Authenticator** - Simple and reliable
- **1Password** - Combined password manager + 2FA
- **Microsoft Authenticator** - Good for Microsoft users

## Using 2FA

After setup, you'll enter a code when logging in:

1. Enter your email and password
2. Open your authenticator app
3. Enter the 6-digit code
4. Access your account

> [!TIP]
> Codes refresh every 30 seconds. If your code doesn't work, wait for the next one.

## Backup Codes

When you enable 2FA, you receive backup codes. These are single-use codes for emergencies.

- Store them securely (password manager, printed copy)
- Each code can only be used once
- Generate new codes if you run out

> [!CAUTION]
> Keep your backup codes safe. If you lose access to your authenticator and backup codes, account recovery may be difficult.

## Disabling 2FA

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Click **Security**
3. Click **Disable 2FA**
4. Enter your password and a 2FA code
5. Confirm

## Lost Your Phone?

If you lose access to your authenticator:

1. Use a backup code to log in
2. Or contact support with account verification
3. Re-setup 2FA with your new device
